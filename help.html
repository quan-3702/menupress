How to use MenuPress

1. Type your menu

Start from an example or type your own. A line starting with # starts a section (# Drinks). Then one dish per line: Name | price | short description, for example Phở tái | $13.95 | Rare steak. The description is optional. A line starting with > is a small note under the section title.

2. Make it pretty

Choose a design, the paper (Letter, A4, Legal, Half Letter) and 1, 2 or 3 columns. Make it fit on one page makes the text a little smaller when the menu is long. Add the address, phone and opening hours at the bottom.

3. QR code

Tick Show a QR code and type the web address of your website, Google page, online ordering or Wi-Fi details. Guests scan it with the phone camera.

4. Print, picture or TV

Print menu – in the print window choose the same paper size and Margins: None, turn on Background graphics for dark designs. Save picture (Pro) makes a PNG for Facebook, Instagram, Google or delivery apps. Menu board (Pro) makes a 1920×1080 picture for a TV in your shop.

Free and Pro

Free: Classic, Modern and Simple designs, one printed page with a small “Made with MenuPress” line. Pro (one-time purchase, no subscription): all 8 designs, your logo, menus with many pages, pictures and the TV menu board, no line at the bottom.
